# Response to BMC Medical Research Methodology decision letter

**Manuscript:** IONE: Incoherence-Oriented Neutralisation and Extraction for Detecting Hidden Population Structure in Observational Studies
**Submission ID:** 7f49c954-3442-436d-8bdd-ea6aa9468caf
**Editor:** Lan Lan, BMC Medical Research Methodology

---

Dear Dr Lan,

Thank you and the reviewers for the time spent evaluating our manuscript.

We have read the editorial assessment and the four sets of reviewer comments carefully. The reviewers identified several core issues that we agree need to be addressed before the work is suitable for publication:

- The reported bias-reduction values are internally inconsistent and the formula must be reconciled with the tables and text.
- The ground-truth subgroup structure and oracle baselines need a clearer definition that matches the reported ARI values.
- The practical utility of diagnostic metrics (W and C1) is overstated; W should be evaluated with an estimated CATE or clearly labelled as simulation-only, and C1 should be framed as an exploratory, not yet calibrated, diagnostic.
- The subgroup-extraction claims should be toned to match the low ARI values seen in the primary simulation.
- The outcome-informed residual stratification carries a circularity risk that must be discussed and, where possible, quantified.
- The method taxonomy and table numbering need to be made consistent.
- Additional implementation details, a code archive, and clearer handling of the semi-synthetic examples are needed.

Because the decision letter states that BMC Medical Research Methodology is not inviting a revised version, we will not request reconsideration. Instead, we will carry out a major revision that addresses the points above and submit the revised manuscript to another peer-reviewed methods journal.

We appreciate the constructive feedback and believe it will significantly strengthen the manuscript.
