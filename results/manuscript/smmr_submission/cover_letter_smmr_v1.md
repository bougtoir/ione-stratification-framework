We wish to submit our manuscript, **“Coherence diagnostics for hidden effect modification in individual participant data meta-analysis: a simulation benchmark of stratification approaches,”** for consideration by *Statistical Methods in Medical Research*.

**Fit with the journal.**
The manuscript develops and benchmarks descriptive diagnostics for a core medical-statistics problem: hidden effect modification in individual participant data (IPD) meta-analysis. The proposed C1 and W statistics quantify between- and within-stratum treatment-effect heterogeneity, and the simulation systematically compares stratification approaches against established medical-statistics tools (propensity-score stratification, prognostic-score stratification, Gaussian mixture models, outcome-residual stratification and clustering). We therefore believe the work will be of interest to the readership of *Statistical Methods in Medical Research*.

**Revisions since previous submissions.**
Earlier versions were considered by *BMC Medical Research Methodology*, *Research Synthesis Methods* and *Biostatistics*. We have substantially revised the manuscript in response to editorial feedback:
- Reframed the paper as an exploratory sensitivity study, not a workflow claiming to recover hidden subgroups.
- Added empirical null distributions and ROC calibration for C1 and W, and reported that discrimination is weak in the primary scenario.
- Centred C1 and W on their null means to address denominator instability.
- Added a study-level meta-analysis comparator and discussed the residual floor at n = 10 000.
- Explicitly separated detection (diagnostic flags) from subgroup recovery (ARI against a constructed true-Z partition).
- Corrected over-causal language and removed references to earlier versions of the analysis.

All numerical results are generated from the repository scripts; the submission package records the exact commit hash and will be archived on Zenodo before acceptance.
