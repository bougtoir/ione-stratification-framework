# IONE candidate journals: SMMR alternatives + Research Synthesis Methods

Comparison of journals with scope close to *Statistical Methods in Medical Research*, now including *Research Synthesis Methods* (RSM). All figures are approximate and should be re-verified at submission because APCs and impact factors change yearly.

| Journal | Publisher | Latest IF (approx.) | APC (USD) / OA model | Fit / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Statistical Methods in Medical Research* | SAGE | 1.9–2.4 | ~$3,000–$4,000 (SAGE Choice hybrid) | Current primary target; explicitly medical-statistics methods. |
| *Research Synthesis Methods* | Cambridge University Press (from 2025; previously Wiley) | 6.1–8.0 | $2,740 / £1,960 (fully OA) | **New candidate** — methods for systematic reviews/meta-analysis; a possible outlet if IONE is reframed as heterogeneity diagnostics for synthesis. |

## Notes

- **Impact factors** are drawn from 2024/2025 JCR releases and publisher/journal-metric sites (as of 2026-08-03). Clarivate’s official JCR should be checked before final decision.
- **APCs** are list prices for open-access (gold or hybrid) publication; subscription submission without OA is often free except where page charges apply. Institutional transformative agreements may cover APCs.
- **Research Synthesis Methods** moved from Wiley to Cambridge University Press in 2025; verify current submission site and any society-discount eligibility.
- Order above is not a strict ranking; best fit depends on whether IONE is pitched as **medical statistics** (SMMR/SiM/Biostatistics), **causal inference** (JoCI/Epidemiologic Methods), **research-synthesis heterogeneity** (RSM), or **general health-research methodology** (BMC MRM/HSORM).
